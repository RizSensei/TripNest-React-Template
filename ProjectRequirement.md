## 🌄 Concept & Overview

**TripNest — The 6:00 AM View** reimagines how travelers discover and book stays across Nepal. Traditional booking platforms treat hotel rooms as utilitarian commodities—prioritizing square footage, price grids, and standard amenity checkboxes like Wi-Fi or televisions. But travelers don't venture into the Himalayas for generic hotel rooms; they travel for the transformative experience of watching dawn break over the highest mountain ranges on Earth.

**TripNest** flips the traditional booking journey upside down by placing the bedroom window perspective at the absolute center of property discovery. Instead of filtering through text-heavy search forms, guests discover stays based on curated morning views captured at 6:00 AM directly from the bed or private balcony of local Nepal accommodations—spanning alpine teahouses in Namche Bazaar, cliffside eco-lodges in Upper Mustang, lakefront resorts in Pokhara, and jungle canopy retreats in Chitwan.

By marrying high-emotion visual discovery with a streamlined front-end booking flow, TripNest bridges the gap between travel dreaming and instant reservation. It transforms property browsing into an interactive morning preview of Nepal's breathtaking geography, rich culture, and warm hospitality.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Bed-Frame Perspective Hero (Visual Discovery Engine)
- **Full-Bleed Window View**: An edge-to-edge visual canvas framing the morning landscape as seen directly from inside the guest room or balcony.
- **Dynamic Time-of-Day Lighting Toggle**: An interactive slider (`6:00 AM Sunrise` | `12:00 PM Midday` | `5:30 PM Golden Hour`) that lets travelers preview how sunlight shifts across mountain peaks and valleys throughout the day.
- **Himalayan HUD (Heads-Up Display)**: A sleek floating overlay displaying essential geographic and atmospheric data:
  - **Exact Elevation**: e.g., *3,440 m* in Solukhumbu vs. *822 m* in Pokhara.
  - **Peak Visibility**: Direct line-of-sight mountain landmarks (e.g., *Ama Dablam, Mt. Everest, Machapuchare*).
  - **Window Orientation & Sky Clarity**: Facing direction (e.g., *East-Facing Dawn Light*) and morning visibility rating (*98% Clear Sky*).

### 2. Curated 5-Region Morning Deck
A horizontal visual carousel showcasing signature dawn perspectives across Nepal's primary travel destinations:
- **The Everest Window** (*Namche Ridge Lodge*) — Cozy alpine woodhouse overlooking the Khumbu valley mist.
- **The Annapurna Balcony** (*Pokhara Lakefront Resort*) — Serene lake reflection with Machapuchare at sunrise.
- **The Mustang Cliffhouse** (*Upper Mustang Eco-Lodge*) — High-desert canyon vistas framed by ancient Newari wood architecture.
- **The Jungle River Canopy** (*Chitwan Forest Lodge*) — Dawn mist rising over the Rapti River and wildlife sanctuary.
- **The Valley Fog & Langtang Ridge** (*Nagarkot Hilltop Boutique*) — Panoramic Kathmandu valley cloud-sea views.

### 3. Integrated Micro-Booking Journey
- **"Reserve This View" Slide-Up Drawer**: A smooth glassmorphism reservation panel triggered directly from any view card.
- **Transparent Room Highlights**: Direct breakdown of stay features, including private balcony access, local heating (bukhari stoves), and included Himalayan breakfasts.
- **Seamless 2-Step Checkout**: A crisp mock booking journey taking users from date/guest selection to checkout, payment preview, and instant booking confirmation.

### 4. Local Host Storytelling & Authenticity
- **"Meet Your Host" Profiles**: Highlights local Sherpa families, Newari artisans, and sanctuary lodge operators to foster human connection prior to booking.
- **Cultural & Regional Add-Ons**: Showcases authentic stay perks like guided morning heritage walks, traditional tea preparation, or monastery sunrise passes.

### 5. Saved Vistas & Wishlist
- **One-Tap Vista Collection**: Travelers can bookmark their favorite morning perspectives to a personalized wishlist page for easy comparison and future trip planning.
